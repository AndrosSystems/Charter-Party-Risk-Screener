It does not constitute legal advice, maritime legal advice, professional consultancy, contractual interpretation, or a determination of the validity, enforceability, effect, suitability or risk of any charter party, clause, fixture or transaction.

## No professional relationship

Use of the application does not create a lawyer-client, consultant-client, fiduciary or other professional relationship. The application should be used as a structured review aid and not as a substitute for qualified professional judgment.

## Automated and AI-generated output

Results are generated from automated text screening, user-provided information and, where enabled, AI-assisted analysis. Outputs may be incomplete, inaccurate, outdated, affected by extraction errors, or misleading where contractual context is missing.

A finding marked **Found**, **Possible** or **Not found** is a text-screening result only. It is not a legal conclusion. In particular, **Not found** does not establish that an issue is absent from the agreement or its legal effect, and a detected term does not establish that the relevant risk has been adequately allocated.

AI-generated comments are hypotheses, review prompts and summaries only. They may contain errors, omit material issues or misinterpret contractual language.

## Complete contractual context required

The significance of any wording depends on the complete charter party, recap, incorporated forms and riders, amendments, bills of lading, governing law, factual circumstances, trade, vessel, cargo, counterparties, sanctions exposure, regulatory regime and negotiations between the parties.

Material decisions — including fixing, signing, performance, withdrawal, termination, refusal of orders, deviation, sanctions compliance, cargo handling, payment, claims or dispute action — should be reviewed by appropriately qualified maritime legal and commercial professionals and handled under the user's approved organisational procedures.
