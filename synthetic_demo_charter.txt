Governing law and dispute resolution:
This agreement shall be governed by English law and disputes shall be referred to arbitration in London.

Trading:
The vessel shall be employed between safe ports and safe berths within agreed trading limits.

Payment:
Hire shall be paid every 15 days in advance.

Off-hire:
In the event of loss of time due to breakdown of machinery preventing the full working of the vessel, hire shall cease for the time thereby lost.

Bunkers:
Charterers shall supply bunkers meeting the agreed fuel specification and quantities will be settled on delivery and redelivery.

War risks:
The parties shall review war-risk exposure before orders to areas affected by hostilities or piracy.

Sanctions:
Neither party shall be required to perform a trade that would place it in breach of applicable sanctions.

Redelivery:
The vessel shall be redelivered within the agreed range and period.
